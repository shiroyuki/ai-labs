For all image generation and character visualisation tasks, apply the "Final Fantasy Tactics Artistic Visualisation" specification.

Use non-photorealistic, hand-painted Japanese tactical fantasy RPG aesthetics inspired by classic Final Fantasy Tactics illustrations.

Mandatory elements:
- Delicate ink-and-pencil outlines.
- Soft watercolour and gouache shading.
- Stylised anime-fantasy anatomy and faces.
- Elegant medieval fantasy costumes.
- Muted colours with carefully controlled accents.
- Hand-painted magic and combat effects.
- Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D rendering, or realistic concept-art appearance.

Preserve all previously established character specifications and approved designs.

When "all motion ranges" is requested, include portraits, front/back standing, sitting, walking, running, jumping, combat stances, normal attacks, special attacks, basic magic, advanced magic, and ultimate attacks.
